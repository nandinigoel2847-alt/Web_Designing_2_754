IMPORTANCE OF MENTAL HEALTH

  1. Daily Life and Personal Well-Being

      Less isolation: Good mental health stops people from feeling lonely and pushed away.

        Heart health: High stress can hurt the heart, while good mental health lowers blood pressure.
        Better sleep: Good mental balance helps you fall asleep and rest well.

        Happy life: It lets you enjoy your daily routine and feel good inside.
